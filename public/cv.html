Publications & Manuscripts
Imitator: Multimodal Sign Language Model
SPRINGER 2026 SimBIG 2025 · International Conf. on Information Management & Big Data
Velasquez Borasino, C.; Mancusi Barreda, G.; Vilchez Marin, R.
Proposed a gloss-free multimodal architecture aligning 2D skeletal keypoint sequences with a pretrained LLM (LLaMA) embedding space using cross-attention with learnable token queries, rotary positional embeddings, and a lightweight conv-transformer encoder. Stable cross-linguistic alignment achieved: validation loss ≈ 8.0×10⁻⁴ under 5-fold CV.
Learning Motion-Based Embeddings for Sign Language Retrieval
IN PREP Manuscript · Submission planned 2026
Velasquez Borasino, C.
Transformer-based embedding model for similarity retrieval in the official Peruvian Sign Language (LSP) digital dictionary. Replaces label-based search with cosine similarity over learned motion embeddings (DTW weak supervision + Triplet Loss). Top-5: 77.8% · Top-10: 88.0% on LSA64.
